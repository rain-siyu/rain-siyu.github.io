Siyu Peng 🍀 彭思雨

Researching in 🔍 AI governance, 🤖 human-computer interaction, and 👥 intelligent communication.

Welcome and thank you for visiting my site! I'm currently a second-year PhD student in Division of Emerging Interdisciplinary Areas (EMIA) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Hong Kong, China. My main-supervisor is Prof. Sirui Han, and co-supervisor is Prof. Yike Guo. I do interdisciplinary research in the fields of AI governance, human-computer interaction, and intelligent communication. I am also interested in data visualization, data journalism, and digital humanities.

I received my master's degree in Journalism and Communications from the School of Communication at East China Normal University (ECNU), Shanghai, China, supervised by Prof. Zhiyong Wu. I received my bachelor of art degree from Shanghai Normal University (SHNU), with a minor bachelor of finance degree from East China University of Science and Technology (ECUST).
